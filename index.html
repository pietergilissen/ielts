IELTS Band 7 self-check

  • Task Achievement / Response: Answer every part and make the overview or position clear.
  • Development: Explain main ideas and support them with relevant detail.
  • Coherence: Make the progression easy to follow.
  • Vocabulary: Use precise, natural language.
  • Grammar: Use a range of structures with good control.
