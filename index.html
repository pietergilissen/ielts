IELTS Academic Writing Practice

Designed for intensive preparation for a Band 7.0 Writing target. Use the 20-minute limit for Task 1 and 40-minute limit for Task 2.

How to use this page

  1. A fresh random Task 1 and Task 2 are selected every time the page is opened.
  2. Set the timer before starting.
  3. Write without looking things up or using a translator.
  4. Use the live word counter to check your length.
  5. After finishing, review grammar, task response, paragraphing and vocabulary.
Target: Task 1 = at least 150 words. Task 2 = at least 250 words. Aim for quality rather than simply writing more.

Basic IELTS score check

Rule-based practice estimate using the four IELTS Writing criteria. This is not an official IELTS score and cannot replace a human examiner.

IELTS Band 7 self-check

  • Task Response: Did I answer every part of the question? Is my position clear throughout?
  • Development: Does every main idea have an explanation and a specific example or consequence?
  • Coherence: Can the reader follow my argument without effort?
  • Vocabulary: Is my vocabulary precise and natural rather than unnecessarily complicated?
  • Grammar: Do I use a mixture of simple and complex structures? Are many sentences completely error-free?
  • Accuracy: Did I check articles, plurals, verb endings, subject–verb agreement, prepositions and spelling?
